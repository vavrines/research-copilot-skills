# Example 01 — From capability-building to scientific proposal

## Trigger
> 当前方案太像能力建设项目。希望加强科学性，但仍保留实验—模拟—数据融合的总体方向。题目、目标和研究内容怎么改？

## Mode
`proposal-master` — Diagnose + Architect + Red-team

## Key judgment
The problem is not insufficient technical content; it is that the proposal describes **what system will be built** before explaining **what unresolved scientific question makes that system necessary**.

## Pattern to reuse
1. Separate strategic/application need from the scientific bottleneck.
2. Identify the latent scientific variables or mechanisms that cannot be directly observed/resolved.
3. Recast the project around an inference/modeling question rather than a platform-delivery statement.
4. Make the platform or integrated capability the consequence of solving the scientific problem, not the scientific problem itself.
5. Re-check that every objective is foreshadowed by the stated bottleneck.

A strong three-level spine is often:
**uncertain state/condition determination → physics–data cooperative modeling → cross-condition credible prediction/validation**.

## Ideal response shape
- One-paragraph diagnosis of why the current version reads as capability construction.
- Revised central scientific question.
- Three differentiated research contents with non-overlapping roles.
- Revised objective paragraph that closes the loop with the background.
- 2–3 title directions, avoiding cosmetic word substitution.
- Reviewer red-team: “Why is this science rather than integration?” and the repair.

## Failure to avoid
- Adding words such as “mechanism”, “theory”, or “AI” to the title without changing the internal logic.
- Creating more work packages to look more scientific.
