# Shared Research Rubric

Use this rubric when a task asks for diagnosis, comparison, prioritization, or red-team assessment.

Score each axis 0–2 unless a different scale is requested.

1. **Question clarity**
   - 0: broad topic or capability statement.
   - 1: identifiable problem, but scope or variables remain loose.
   - 2: precise scientific/technical question with explicit boundary conditions.

2. **Necessity / gap**
   - 0: method is optional; existing approaches likely suffice.
   - 1: plausible gap, weakly demonstrated.
   - 2: clear failure mode or missing capability that makes the proposed approach necessary.

3. **Claim–evidence match**
   - 0: central claims exceed evidence.
   - 1: partially supported, important tests missing.
   - 2: every central claim has a direct analysis, experiment, or comparison.

4. **Methodological novelty**
   - 0: straightforward combination or implementation.
   - 1: meaningful adaptation/integration.
   - 2: new principle, formulation, or mechanism with generalizable value.

5. **Validation strength**
   - 0: toy-only or self-consistency evidence.
   - 1: credible benchmark evidence.
   - 2: discriminating tests against strong baselines plus practical/representative validation.

6. **Closure / deliverability**
   - 0: goals, tasks, metrics, and deliverables are disconnected.
   - 1: mostly aligned with gaps.
   - 2: explicit one-to-one or many-to-one closure and feasible verification path.

### Interpretation
- 10–12: strong core; focus on execution and presentation.
- 7–9: viable but needs one or two structural repairs.
- 4–6: interesting direction but not yet a convincing paper/proposal.
- 0–3: reframe the question before investing heavily.
