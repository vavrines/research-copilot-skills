# Example 04 — Make a project deck look scientifically deep without adding clutter

## Trigger
> 领导认为现在偏能力建设，前沿科学性不足。PPT 怎么改才能让“科学问题—研究内容—目标”形成闭环？

## Mode
`slide-storyteller` — Slide redesign

## Key judgment
Scientific depth on slides comes from the **question structure and evidence chain**, not from denser equations or adding a “机理” label.

## Pattern to reuse
Rebuild the middle of the deck around:
1. observed discrepancy/uncertainty;
2. why existing experiment/simulation alone cannot resolve it;
3. one explicit scientific question;
4. three research contents that each remove one part of the uncertainty;
5. technical route showing information flow;
6. measurable outcomes that directly answer the question.

## Ideal response shape
Return a page-level surgery plan:
- pages to keep;
- pages to merge/delete;
- replacement page titles;
- one recommended mechanism/flow schematic;
- revised talk transition around the scientific-question page.

## Failure to avoid
- Adding more background pages or equations without changing the decision logic.
