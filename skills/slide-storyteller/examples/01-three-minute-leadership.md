# Example 01 — Three-minute leadership briefing

## Trigger
> 需要给领导写 3 分钟讲稿。当前稿背景很多，但完全没体现研究内容；既要讲国家需求和装置意义，也不能显得只是能力建设。

## Mode
`slide-storyteller` — Funding/program leadership profile

## Key judgment
In three minutes, background can justify urgency but cannot consume the whole talk. The audience still needs one compact explanation of **what scientific work will be done**.

## Pattern to reuse
A reliable 3-minute allocation:
- 25–30%: why now / strategic need;
- 20–25%: current scientific bottleneck;
- 30–35%: core idea + three research contents;
- 15–20%: expected change/capability and why the team can deliver.

Compress each research content to one verb + one scientific object + one outcome.

## Ideal response shape
A continuous script with clear transitions, not page-reading. Include the research contents once, even if only in one compact sentence.

## Failure to avoid
- Spending 80% of time on “why important”.
- Listing three tasks with no causal relation to the stated bottleneck.
