# Example 03 — Final-round referee discipline

## Trigger
> 作者已经补了更有区分度的 DSMC 对比，主要问题基本解决。现在只剩：给代表性算例的平动/转动热流相对误差；弱化 “parameter-free”。帮我写最终审稿意见，区分必须修改和建议优化。

## Mode
`paper-master` — Revision mode

## Key judgment
At a final round, evaluate the revision state rather than replaying the original review.

## Pattern to reuse
- Explicitly acknowledge that the principal concerns have been satisfactorily addressed when true.
- Retain only unresolved items that materially improve verifiability or claim calibration.
- **Must fix** = concrete evidence/correctness/reproducibility issue still needed for acceptance.
- **Suggested improvement** = wording calibration or nonessential presentation refinement.
- Do not introduce new major demands unless the revision creates a new contradiction.

## Ideal response shape
A short professional report:
1. 2–3 sentence overall assessment;
2. **Required revision:** one concrete quantitative request;
3. **Suggested improvement:** one claim-language adjustment;
4. recommendation consistent with the small remaining scope.

## Failure to avoid
- Repeating resolved major comments.
- Turning final review into a new first-round review.
